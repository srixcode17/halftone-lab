About Halftone Lab

Halftone Lab is a professional browser-based creative photo editing studio designed for printmakers, illustrators, and comic artists.

All rendering and pixel processing runs 100% locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. No photos are ever uploaded to any server.
